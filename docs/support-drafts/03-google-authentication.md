# Google authentication troubleshooting

Status: Draft — not approved for customer-support retrieval.

Trace provides a Google sign-in option through its authentication flow. Successful
Google authentication depends on the team's Google and authentication-project
configuration. Automated fixture checks do not prove that real Google login is ready.

If sign-in is cancelled or the callback fails, use the displayed safe guidance and
start a fresh sign-in attempt. Keep the intended account in mind when choosing a
Google identity. A different external identity can create a different Trace account.

This draft does not promise automatic merging of different accounts or manual
Google account linking. Do not share callback URLs, passwords or authentication
credentials in a support conversation.

Owner review needed: confirm real Google login and approve a support destination.
