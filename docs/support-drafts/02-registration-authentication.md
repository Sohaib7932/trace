# Registration and authentication

Status: Draft — not approved for customer-support retrieval.

Trace includes Sign up, Sign in, Forgot password, Reset password and Account pages.
Email registration requires confirmation before the account is treated as verified.
The recovery request gives a general response rather than disclosing whether an
email address has an account. An invalid or expired recovery link needs a new request.

Guest learning remains available. An account can explicitly claim accessible guest
progress. Starting separate account learning first can create a different history;
a claim conflict preserves both histories rather than merging them automatically.

Real registration, confirmation email delivery and recovery depend on the team's
authentication configuration. This draft does not certify a deployed service.
Never include passwords or authentication credentials in a support question.

Owner review needed: verify real account and email flows before publishing guidance.
