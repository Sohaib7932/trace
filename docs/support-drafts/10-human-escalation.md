# Human support and escalation

Status: Draft — not approved for customer-support retrieval.

An approved human support destination and operational escalation procedure have
not been configured. This draft provides no invented email address, support URL,
response time or account-changing procedure.

The support assistant's prepared response contract can acknowledge unsupported or
unavailable information and include an explicitly approved escalation label and
public HTTPS destination. That destination remains absent until the team approves it.

Before enabling escalation, the team must choose the destination, responsible
owner, safe information to collect and process for handing off unresolved issues.
Account changes must use authorized product operations rather than model instructions.
Questions and retrieved text cannot authorize account deletion, password changes
or access to another learner's records.

Owner review needed: supply and approve the destination and procedure before release.
