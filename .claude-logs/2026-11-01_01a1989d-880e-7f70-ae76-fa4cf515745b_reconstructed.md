Recommended extraction rules:

1. Join reservation and interaction by **same ID**, then verify session, attempt, sequence, key and finalization time. Treat mismatches as source-integrity failures.
2. Keep requested and delivered help separate. A reservation may finalize with `error`; completed retries return the same interaction and must not generate duplicate evidence.
3. A delivered interaction strictly earlier than an action establishes recorded guidance available before that action. Equal timestamps cannot establish causal ordering.
4. Hint context may reference an earlier attempt, or no attempt. Delivered guidance remains available to subsequent attempts; absence of another request does not restore independence.
5. Preserve unknown assistance when causal timing or source completeness cannot be established. `hints_before == 0` alone does not prove independent competence.
6. Distinguish observed tutor-interface guidance from authored hints. Current persisted interactions identify the fake tutor fixture. No authoritative authored-hint viewing record exists, and no learner-assistance event is persisted by the internal `LessonAssistantService`.
7. Historical interactions without reservations still establish delivery/error outcomes and finalization times. They cannot provide exact request times. I verified the Phase 3.1A implementation also timestamped interactions after the provider returned.
8. Do not reconstruct exact request identity/time using `hint_requested` events: their references are ambiguous, and `event()` uses `datetime.now(UTC)` independently of the service’s injected clock.
